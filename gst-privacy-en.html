Game Studio Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

We respect your privacy. The developer of "Game Studio Tycoon" (the "App") does not collect personal information directly. The Google services below process some information for ads, purchases, cloud saves, error reports and usage statistics.

1. Personal information

The developer does not collect your name, email, phone number, location or similar personal information, and does not send it to any server of its own. The App has no sign-up of its own; signing in to Google Play Games is optional (section 5).

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress (save data) and settings (sound, music, language) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or third parties and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

"Create backup code" in Settings only shows your save data as text on screen. You decide where to keep it; the App never sends it anywhere.

3. Notifications

The App schedules reminders (daily check-in, offline earnings) locally on your device. No external server is used. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App offers rewarded ads that play only when you tap a button. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and Google may collect and process your advertising ID, device information, IP address and ad interaction data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

In regions that require consent (such as the EEA), a consent form is shown when the App starts. If you do not consent, non-personalized ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Google > Ads).

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Cloud save (Google Play Games Services)

If you sign in to Google Play Games in Settings, your save data is stored in Google Play Games Saved Games so you can continue on another device. Google processes your player ID and gamer name for this. If you do not sign in, this feature is off. Google Privacy Policy

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The "Remove ads" item is bought through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google and never reach the developer. The App only checks your purchase history with Google Play to confirm the purchase.

7. Error reports and usage statistics (Firebase Crashlytics & Google Analytics for Firebase)

If the App crashes or hits an error, the error details, device model, OS version, app version, app language and an installation identifier (Crashlytics installation ID) may be sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. This information is not used to identify you. Firebase privacy information

To improve the App we also collect usage statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase: tutorial progress, key actions such as releasing a game, hiring or watching a rewarded ad, the app language and progress stage, and app launches and screen views, together with device model, OS version, approximate country/region, an app instance ID and the advertising ID. We do not collect names, email addresses or other directly identifying information, and this data is not used to identify you. You can reset your advertising ID in device settings (Google > Ads).

Recent errors are also kept on your device and can be viewed or copied from "🐞 Error log" in Settings.

8. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not serve personalized ads directed at children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update and its effective date on this page.
